# Godrej Regent Park (Kada Agrahara, off Sarjapur Road) — investment review

Prepared 26 Sep 2026 from the K-RERA disclosure set in this repository (97 files: sanctioned drawings, commencement certificate, agreement for sale, allotment letter, sale deed, encumbrance certificates, conversion orders, khata, water NOC, cost certificates) plus public web sources. The project's own 360 site and the Godrej site were blocked by this environment's network policy, so nothing was read from them.

## 1. Verdict

**Conditional yes.** The paperwork is unusually complete and the title/approval risk is low. The economics are only fair: you are paying a top-of-micro-market price (about ₹10,900 per sq ft of super built-up area, roughly ₹18,400 per sq ft of RERA carpet) for a home that is five years from possession, on a 40 ft approach road, with tanker/panchayat water, and with roughly 24 more acres of Godrej's own future towers coming up next door. Base-case returns look like 7–10% IRR with 2.5–3.5% gross rental yield after 2031. That is acceptable for a patient, low-risk-appetite buyer, not a bargain.

**On the specific offer (Tower A 2401 at ₹1.355 Cr):** the cost sheet confirms it is all-in including GST and other charges, at about ₹10,120 per sq ft of SBA before GST. It is a west-balcony 2 BHK facing the open land where later Godrej towers are most likely. Not a corner unit; the four corners are 3 BHKs. The better choices at the same money are the east-balcony 2 BHKs in Tower B (B-2805 first), and the better choice overall, if the budget allows about ₹2.13 Cr with registration, is the north-east 3-toilet 3 BHK B-x04 at ₹2.0 Cr all-in, which works out to the same per-sq-ft rate. Details in sections 8 and 9.

## 3. What surrounds the towers (from the sanctioned site plan)

The two towers stand in a line running roughly north–south, so every apartment looks east or west. Tower A is the southern tower, Tower B the northern one, with the clubhouse between them and the pool on the east side.

- **North (Tower B end):** 12.19 m public road, then the 3,761 sq m park and the civic-amenity site, then the internal 12.19 m road to the main gate. This is the only side with a guaranteed permanent open view.
- **East (balconies of units 05/06 and the 3 BHK corners 04/07):** driveway and pool, 16 m setback, 12.19 m public road, an 8-gunta footpath kharab strip, then the villa layout seen in the 360 views (individual three-storey villas on plots, some plots still vacant; Sree Narayana Nagar / Modern Spaaces Soulace area). A plotted villa layout cannot take a high-rise under Anekal Planning Authority norms, so this is the protected side. It is not a JR project; JRC Palladio is on the Sarjapur–Attibele road several km away.
- **West (balconies of units 01/02 and corners 03/08):** 16 m setback, 12.19 m public road, then Sy. 27/7 (open) and the Kommasandra village boundary. Open and buildable; the most likely site for Godrej's later towers.
- **South (Tower A end):** 12.19 m road, then Sy. 27/6 and the B. Hosahalli village boundary. Open and buildable.

**The adjoining open land will almost certainly get towers, and Godrej will build them.** Agreement for sale recital K: "The Promoter has acquired certain lands adjacent to the Larger Property and is in the process of acquiring additional adjoining lands." Recitals R and S and clause 1.3 make you consent in advance to more towers, shared amenities and a revised layout. Channel-partner sites describe the scheme as "32 acres total, Phase 1 = 8.07 acres". The land Godrej bought was the Modern Spaaces "Urban Oasis" 34-acre villa land. Expect 4–6 more towers of similar height over the next decade; the minimum gap to a future tower across a road is about 44 m (16 m + 12.19 m + 16 m).

## 4. Approach road

- Access is from Sompura Gate on Sarjapur Main Road, south along Kada Agrahara Road through Kada Agrahara village, then west on the Kada Agrahara–Hosahalli road. Roughly 2.5–3 km from the main road (estimate from the plan's coordinates; drive it at 9 am and 7 pm before deciding).
- The road in front of the layout is an **existing 12.20 m (40 ft) road, "widening to 18.00 m (60 ft)"** per the Anekal development plan (DWG 1, AFS recital H). The panchayat NOC also records 40 ft access. Godrej has relinquished its own frontage; the other landowners have not, and no funded timeline exists.
- The last 110 m from that road to the gate is a 12.19 m public road Godrej built and handed over, with 12.19 m roads on all four sides of the building plot. Within the layout, access is fine.
- Verdict: adequate for 534 units, not "too narrow" by Sarjapur standards, but the village stretch will feel congested once Godrej's later phases, Puravankara's East-End and the villa projects fill up. The 60 ft widening is the upside if it happens.

## 5. Is it waterlogged or low-lying?

Nothing in the documents suggests so, but I could not verify ground levels independently (elevation services were blocked).

- The sanctioned plan shows no nala, rajakaluve or lake buffer on or beside the site. The hatched strips on the east are "kalu dari kharab" (footpath kharab), not drains. The site is bounded by roads on all sides.
- The STP drawing gives the finished ground floor at RL +904.0 m and basement 3 at +892.9 m. Public listings put Kada Agrahara village at about 874 m. If both are mean-sea-level figures, the site sits on higher ground than the village and the Yamare lake catchment to the north-east.
- Regional caveat: Yamare Gram Panchayat (the same panchayat) was accused in the press of building a road over a rajakaluve near Yamare lake, and the wider Sarjapur belt floods at low points after cloudbursts. The three-basement design means stormwater pumping matters; the SEIAA clearance (7 May 2026) would have required a hydrology study. Ask Godrej for the contour/hydrology drawings from the EC application, and visit the site and the village road right after a heavy rain.

## 6. Water supply (bigger practical risk than flooding)

The project is outside BWSSB's Cauvery network. Supply is panchayat/borewell/tanker, and the AFS puts the cost into maintenance. This is normal for Anekal Planning Authority projects, but it means higher maintenance bills and periodic shortages in summer, which tenants notice. Cauvery Stage V does not cover this belt; do not assume it will by 2031.

## 7. What is likely to come up around it

- Godrej's own later phases on the remaining ~24 acres (see section 3).
- Puravankara "Codename East-End", 6 acres near Sompura Gate, 2 and 3 BHK marketed from ₹1.10–1.60 Cr, i.e. cheaper competition at the same junction; Purva Silver Sky (Kada Agrahara, larger units ₹2.45–6.59 Cr).
- Villas and plots: Modern Spaaces Soulace (26.5 acres), NCC Urban Green Province, S2 Avenues, Sree Narayana Nagar plots; Sowparnika Sai Krishna apartments on Kada Agrahara Road.
- Infrastructure: Sarjapur Main Road widening to 4 lanes in stretches; Satellite Town Ring Road Hoskote–Attibele section nearly complete a few km east; Namma Metro Phase 3A (Hebbal–Sarjapur, 37.8 km, 28 stations) approved by the state, DPR with the Centre, construction from about 2027 and opening around 2033, with the Sarjapur terminal a few km from the site; Infosys' 202-acre Sarjapur campus (long pending) and Wipro's 50-acre campus; KIADB has proposed a 647-acre industrial park at Sarjapur. Schools already nearby: Indus International, Greenwood High, Oakridge; Allen institute at Sompura Gate.
- Net effect: demand drivers are real but slow (metro is 7 years out), while supply in the immediate pocket is large and cheaper than Godrej.

## 8. Price, sales and the ₹1.355 Cr offer

**What Godrej paid:** about ₹108 Cr for 8.07 acres (₹13.4 Cr/acre). Land works out to roughly ₹1,400 per sq ft of saleable area; total project cost of ₹633 Cr is about ₹8,100 per sq ft of saleable area. At ₹11,000–12,000 per sq ft sale prices the margin is around 30%, so Godrej has room to discount later phases or slow-moving units, which caps resale upside for early buyers until the project sells out.

**Launch pricing:** 2 BHK (1,240 sq ft) listed from ₹1.44 Cr (≈ ₹11,600/sq ft); 3 BHK 2T from ₹1.80 Cr; 3 BHK 3T (1,870 sq ft) from ₹2.16 Cr. Godrej's own listing page now advertises "from ₹1.23 Cr", so ₹1.23 Cr is the real floor for a low-floor 2 BHK. Your ₹1.355 Cr for a 24th-floor unit is about 10% above that floor, which is what normal floor-rise plus a location premium would add. In other words, the "started at 1.44/1.46, now 1.355 final" framing is sales choreography rather than a genuine 6–7% cut.

**Units sold:** no public figure. Karnataka RERA requires quarterly progress updates; the first (Jul–Sep 2026) should appear on the RERA project page in October. Ask the sales manager for the live inventory screen for Tower A and B. The speed with which they moved from list to "final" within ten weeks suggests inventory is not tight.

Floor rise absorbed. Same payment plan. Stamp duty, registration and possession-time deposits are extra.

That is the 2 BHK rate with no corner premium, for the only unit type with two protected sides (east over the villa layout, north over the relinquished park), two balconies and three toilets. Registration adds about ₹12.3 lakh, so about ₹2.13 Cr all-in. If instead ₹2.0 Cr is the pre-GST consideration, the total is about ₹2.145 Cr, or ₹10,700 per sq ft SBA, a 6% premium to the 2 BHK; still fair for a corner, but then the 2 BHK is the better value per rupee. Counter at ₹2.0 Cr all-in and treat ₹2.05 Cr all-in as the ceiling.

The two 2 BHK types are the same size. The 01/02 type has 1.7 sq m more RERA carpet on paper because its passage is longer; the 05/06 type has about 4 sq m more room floor (master 3.35×4.02 against 3.35×3.35, living 3.20×3.51 against 3.24×3.20, dining 3.65×2.78 against 3.35×2.17) and a larger balcony. An earlier draft of this review estimated 05/06 as 9% larger in carpet from scaling the sanctioned plan; the official floor plans show that was wrong.

The marketing floor plan uses the same numbering and confirms the orientation (01/02 top row with west balconies and east doors; 05/06 bottom row with east balconies and west doors). It labels the outer corners (A-07/08, B-03/04) "3 BHK Luxe" and the corners beside the clubhouse (A-03/04, B-07/08) "3 BHK PRM"; on the drawing the outer corners are the larger three-toilet units (three bedrooms of 3.43 × 4.02, 3.42 × 4.02 and 3.43 × 3.86) and the inner ones the smaller two-toilet units (master 3.35 × 4.65, two bedrooms 3.35 × 3.35). Both 2 BHK variants are marketed as "2 BHK Luxe" despite the size difference. The 20th and 30th floors are refuge floors.

So for **A-2401, if the sales numbering matches the architect's**: it is the smaller of the two 2 BHK types; its main door faces east (which is probably what "east-facing" means in the pitch); its living room, both bedrooms and balcony face **west**, over the west road toward the villa side; it is "corner-like" only in the sense that its south wall opens onto the open-to-sky cutout. It is not one of the four true corner units on the floor, which are all 3 BHKs.

**Numbering confirmed.** The official floor plans and the A-2401 cost sheet confirm that the sales numbers follow the architect's numbering: 01/02 are the west-balcony 2 BHKs (72.24 sq m carpet, 1,246.75 sq ft SBA) and 05/06 the east-balcony 2 BHKs (70.56 sq m carpet, 1,239.73 sq ft SBA). A-2401 has a west-facing balcony toward the open land; "east-facing" referred to its door. Neither type is a true corner unit.

**Which is best for an investor (2 BHK budget):**

1. **First choice among 2 BHKs: a 05/06-type in Tower B, floors 15–28.** Same size and price band as 01/02, larger rooms and balcony, morning sun, and the east balcony looks over the pool toward the villa layout, which cannot take a high-rise. Main door faces west, which some Vastu-minded buyers dislike; the protected view is worth more on resale than the door direction.
0. **Best unit in the project if the budget stretches to about ₹2.13 Cr all-in: B-x04**, the north-east 3-toilet 3 BHK, at ₹2.0 Cr all-in (see section 8). Floor matters little for it: the villas opposite are three storeys and the park view is at ground level, so the 10th floor upward is fine.
2. **Second choice: a 01/02-type 2 BHK in Tower B, similar floors,** if you want an east-facing door for resale and you value the villa community on the west as a semi-permanent low-rise view (villas rarely get redeveloped within 10–15 years). Accept afternoon sun.
3. **If budget stretches:** B-x03 or B-x04, the 3 BHK corners at Tower B's north end, are the only units with a guaranteed permanent open outlook (park and civic-amenity site). About ₹2.16 Cr though, and 3 BHK yields are lower.

A-2401 specifically: fine unit, but pay no premium for "corner" or "east-facing", and compare its price against a B-2405/2406 cost sheet on the same day.

**Switching to B-2705 / B-2706 / B-2805 (offered as available):** these are the larger east-balcony type facing the villa layout, so the switch is right. Order: B-2805 first (unit 05 is the north-side unit of the pair: north-facing side notch, east-plus-north open sides, next to the park corner, farthest from the clubhouse; one floor higher for one step of floor rise), then B-2705, then B-2706 (side notch faces south toward the clubhouse gap). Three open units in one stack also means there is room to negotiate: ask for the chosen unit at the same all-in price as the A-2401 cheque, or for GST or deposits to be absorbed, with floor-rise and any view charge itemised. Have the booking amended, not cancelled and rebooked. Tower A's south end and the whole west and east frontages face land that Godrej says it is buying, so no 2 BHK view is protected; only the north end is.

## 10. Contract points worth knowing (agreement for sale, revised after RERA queries)

- Payment: 5% booking, 5% in 15 days, 10% in 90 days, then construction-linked to 34th floor, 10% at flooring, 10% at OC application, 10% at possession notice.
- Delay: refund with interest at SBI MCLR + 2% if possession is not offered by 31 Jul 2031, subject to force majeure; expect Godrej to use the full window.
- Cancellation: forfeit 10% of consideration plus brokerage and GST; refund of the rest without interest in 60 days.
- Area variation beyond ±3% is adjusted at the agreed rate in the last instalment.
- Holding charges of ₹500 per sq m per month if you do not take possession within 30 days of offer.
- All present and future FSI/TDR stays with Godrej; you consent to added towers, changed layouts and shared amenities with future phases (recitals K, R, S; clause 1.3).
- Defect liability 5 years from possession intimation.
- RERA made Godrej delete transfer/assignment fees, separate car-park pricing and management fees, so the registered agreement should not contain them; check yours.

## 11. Before you pay anything

1. Ask for A-2401 marked on sanctioned drawing 12 of 13 and the RERA carpet area in sq m from the allotment letter annexure.
2. Get an itemised cost sheet showing base rate, floor rise, PLC, GST, other charges, and a matching sheet for B-2405/2406.
3. Ask for the tower-wise sold inventory and check the RERA quarterly update after 30 September.
4. Ask which survey numbers form the "Additional Lands" in recital K and where the later towers will stand relative to your unit.
5. Ask for the SEIAA environmental clearance letter and the site contour/hydrology drawing.
6. Confirm the home-loan bank has issued a project approval (its legal team will have re-verified the court-decree conveyance in the title chain).
7. Drive Sompura Gate to the site at peak hours and after a heavy rain.
